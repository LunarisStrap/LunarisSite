Features

Everything the default launcher won't let you do.

LunarisStrap wraps the official Roblox client and adds a layer of control on top — nothing more, nothing hidden.

FastFlag editor

Search and toggle Roblox's client flags through a readable UI — no manual JSON editing, no guesswork.

Themes & icons

Swap the loading screen, splash art, and app icon. Apply community themes or build your own.

Account safety

Works entirely with the local install. Your login and cookie never pass through LunarisStrap.

Discord Rich Presence

Show what you're playing, join buttons included, without a third-party bot in the loop.

Custom sounds & mods

Drop in replacement sounds, cursors, and fonts. LunarisStrap reapplies them after every update.

Always up to date

Tracks Roblox's own update channel automatically, so your client never falls behind.

Installation

Four short phases, then you're in.

Getting set up looks like a lunar cycle — new, to first quarter, to full. No config files to hand-edit.

  • New moon

    Download the installer

    Grab the latest LunarisStrap.exe from the download section below.

  • First quarter

    Run it once

    LunarisStrap detects your existing Roblox install, or sets up a fresh one automatically.

  • Waxing gibbous

    Pick your setup

    Open Flags, Themes or Mods and choose what you want — everything's optional.

  • Full moon

    Play

    Launch Roblox straight from LunarisStrap from now on — your settings persist between sessions.

C:\Users\you> LunarisStrap.exe --player

Ready when you are.

One installer, a couple of minutes, and your Roblox client is yours to shape.
